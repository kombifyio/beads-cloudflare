# Code of conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
Everyone who takes part in its issues, pull requests, discussions and other spaces is expected to follow it.

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to the maintainers through
[GitHub private reporting](https://github.com/kombifyio/beads-cloudflare/security/advisories/new), marked as a
conduct report, or to the moderators of the space where it happened. The maintainers review every report, keep the
reporter's identity confidential and act according to the enforcement guidelines of the Covenant.
